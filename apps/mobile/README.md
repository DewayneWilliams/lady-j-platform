# Lady J Salon & Day Spa

A responsive Expo app for private salon bookings, professional workshops, boutique shopping and owner-only business management. Light and dark themes use readable foreground accents and full-opacity disabled labels on neutral surfaces with the Lady J vibrant magenta and gold accents, with Playfair Display for the salon wordmark and Inter for readable headings and body text.

## App structure

- `app/index.tsx` redirects to `/home`; `/[page]` resolves customer screens; `/owner` is a separate guarded workspace.
- `components/salon` holds reusable themed UI and feature screens. Existing `components/ui` primitives provide base controls.
- `lib/salon/SalonProvider.tsx` manages the live catalog, persistent auth session, private account records, bag and selected services.
- `lib/salon/client.ts` initializes Supabase from environment variables. `integrations.ts` defines trusted server adapter contracts and explicit unconfigured provider states.
- `supabase/migrations` contains the development database schema, RLS, seeds and transactional operations.

Every gold client header orders **brand → language selector → theme toggle → BOOK YOUR APPOINTMENT** and remains compact at mobile widths. Customer routes use Expo Router tabs; the owner workspace uses its registered five-tab navigator. Both pale-gold `#F9E4C8` bottom bars reserve safe-area space and keep equally sized high-contrast dark icon/label columns without overlays. Development Test Mode adds a slim labeled client/owner switch below the header; outside DEVELOPMENT, guests follow the existing registration flow and owner routes remain authenticated. Contact WhatsApp uses only the configured salon number.

Home ends with a compact verified Lady J Google Maps embed and exact two-line address under the single-line “Find Lady J Salon & Day Spa” heading, without ratings, review cards or Google profile-link copy, followed only by centered business hours and one Terms/Privacy line. The royal-blue complimentary consultation action preserves its authentication destination and opens the secure one-page consultation questionnaire immediately after sign-in or OAuth recovery. Products use the Services card grid geometry; Workshops pairs the approved Academy heading and introduction with an original hands-on classroom photo; Contact uses an original representative reception image and disables social profiles until verified URLs are configured. Development Test Mode is controlled solely by `EXPO_PUBLIC_DEVELOPMENT_TEST_MODE`, is restricted to the DEVELOPMENT build, and provides labeled client/owner switching with local simulations and fictional owner data without weakening production auth or RLS.

## Free customer accounts

Guests may browse service, workshop and boutique descriptions and prices. A free account is required to open private availability, book, add products to the shopping bag or check out. These screens preserve the selected service or checkout destination through sign-up/sign-in. Allowlisted public database views expose active catalog prices while excluding bank instructions and delivery fees; anonymous roles cannot read the base catalog tables or call availability.

Email/password accounts work with Supabase. Google, Facebook and Apple use a real PKCE flow with an Expo WebBrowser popup/authentication session and the `/auth/callback` route. Buttons discover provider availability from Supabase and remain disabled with setup labels until configured. No social credentials are embedded in the app. See **Customer sign-in setup** in `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md` for the exact panel settings and redirect URLs.

Privacy/Terms links and trust language explain free accounts and private records. Marketing is optional, defaults off and is independent of booking. New email accounts save the selected preference; social/email sign-in only opts an existing account in when explicitly checked, never silently opts it out. My Profile supports withdrawal. Preference changes receive a database timestamp. The privacy notice remains subject to the owner's launch review.

Migration 007 adds restricted public catalog views, authenticated availability access and consent timestamps; migration 035 adds active catalog prices to those views. Deploy through the Supabase panel before production uses this flow.

## Discounts

**Business → Discounts** lets the verified owner create and edit percentage offers, use optional promo codes, target all items, one category or one specific service/product/workshop, set an expiry date (end of day in Trinidad) and deactivate an offer. Automatic offers require no code. Customer booking and checkout show a backend-validated quote before submitting; codes are case-insensitive and the best eligible percentage applies per item without stacking. Prices are snapshotted, delivery fees are excluded and booking deposits use the discounted total. **Apply discount** on unpaid pending appointment/order/enrollment records replaces the current discount and adjusts pending payment requests; purchases with received payments are protected. Appointment discounts preserve verified receipts and cannot reduce the total below money already received. Zero restores original prices. Demo workspace offers affect fictional records only.

Migrations 008–009 add discount storage, quoting and payment locking on the development branch. Deploy through the Supabase panel before production uses discounts. Owner authorization still requires the salon owner's account to be assigned in `lj_owners`.

## Received-revenue reports

**Business → Revenue & reports** provides current-month, 7-day, 30-day, year-to-date and custom-date reports in TTD using America/Port_of_Spain. Reports show receipts, actual refunds and net received, grouped by Services, Products and Workshops, with booked service/item details and receipt activity. Deposits count when received; pending and voided requests are excluded. Shared receipts are allocated to booked items in cents; delivery adjustments are separate. New records use receipt/refund timestamps; legacy request-date fallbacks are disclosed. These are cash-received reports, not profit-and-loss accounts.

Download CSV exports every matching item entry for the selected period/category, with receipt IDs, references and date basis, excluding client notes and card details. CSV cells escape quotes and neutralize text formulas. Web downloads directly; iOS/Android use Expo FileSystem and Sharing behind platform-specific modules. Finance source tables paginate past the default 1,000-row database limit. The labeled owner demo posts fictional receipts in memory and exports files visibly marked Demo data.

Migrations 006 and 024 provide timestamped receipts, nullable customer linkage for walk-ins, voided unpaid requests and owner-confirmed balance settlement. Payment complete is available for pending, confirmed and completed appointments with a balance; discounts update the amount due, while existing receipts stay unchanged. Payment confirms a pending appointment without marking its service complete. Exact-balance checks and row locks prevent stale amounts and duplicate receipts. Deploy development changes through the Supabase panel for production.
