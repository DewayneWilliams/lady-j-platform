# Lady J Salon & Day Spa

A responsive Expo app for private salon bookings, professional workshops, boutique shopping and owner-only business management. Light and dark themes use the Lady J vibrant magenta and gold accents with neutral light/dark surfaces, with Playfair Display for the salon wordmark and Inter for readable headings and body text.

## App structure

- `app/index.tsx` redirects to `/home`; `/[page]` resolves customer screens; `/owner` is a separate guarded workspace.
- `components/salon` holds reusable themed UI and feature screens. Existing `components/ui` primitives provide base controls.
- `lib/salon/SalonProvider.tsx` manages the live catalog, persistent auth session, private account records, bag and selected services.
- `lib/salon/client.ts` initializes Supabase from environment variables. `integrations.ts` defines trusted server adapter contracts and explicit unconfigured provider states.
- `supabase/migrations` contains the development database schema, RLS, seeds and transactional operations.

Every gold client header has a single row: **brand → language selector → theme toggle → BOOK YOUR APPOINTMENT**. Mobile controls stay on that row; the rounded rectangular booking button can wrap its label. The English/Spanish selector translates the header and bottom navigation, with its preference held in memory across navigation; page content remains English. The shopping-bag shortcut is in Products. Guests open free registration; signed-in clients open booking. Home has no duplicate hero booking buttons. The Lady J wordmark has no circular monogram. Workshops promote **Lady J International Beauty Academy**. The footer shows **On My Way Plaza, Petit Valley Trinidad and Tobago**. The floating translucent glass bottom bar contains exactly **Home, Services, Products, Workshops, Contact, Account** with refined 26px outline icons, a soft magenta selection and complete labels on mobile, tablet and desktop. Client and owner mobile bars use shared `BottomNavigation` with Expo Blur and light/dark glass tokens. Android SDK 31+ blurs the scrolling `BlurTargetView`; older Android retains a translucent surface. iOS Reduce Transparency switches to an opaque surface. Equal zero-basis flex columns avoid cached widths, align single-line labels and apply bottom safe-area padding once. Scroll content and notices include clearance for the floating capsule. Explicit safe-area edge modes prevent extra top/side padding on mobile web. The single-page build uses `public/index.html` with `viewport-fit=cover` and dynamic viewport height and a runtime visual-viewport listener so browser toolbars and restored pages do not leave the menu below the visible screen. Repeated tab taps refresh the selected screen; client tabs navigate without stacking duplicate routes. The header theme toggle is available at every width. WhatsApp support is available on Contact and configured through `lj_settings.whatsapp_phone` in Business settings. ## Free customer accounts

Guests may browse service, workshop and boutique descriptions. A free account is required to see prices, open the appointment calendar, book, access the shopping bag or check out. These screens preserve the selected service or checkout destination through sign-up/sign-in. Price-free public database views exclude prices, bank instructions and delivery fees; anonymous roles cannot read the priced tables or call availability. Catalog state is cleared on authentication changes so private prices cannot survive sign-out.

Email/password accounts work with Supabase. Google, Facebook and Apple use a real PKCE flow with an Expo WebBrowser popup/authentication session and the `/auth/callback` route. Buttons discover provider availability from Supabase and remain disabled with setup labels until configured. No social credentials are embedded in the app. See **Customer sign-in setup** in `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md` for the exact panel settings and redirect URLs.

Privacy/Terms links and trust language explain free accounts and private records. Marketing is optional, defaults off and is independent of booking. New email accounts save the selected preference; social/email sign-in only opts an existing account in when explicitly checked, never silently opts it out. My Profile supports withdrawal. Preference changes receive a database timestamp. The privacy notice remains subject to the owner's launch review.

Migration 007 adds public price-free views, authenticated price/availability access and consent timestamps on the development branch. Deploy through the Supabase panel before production uses this flow.

## Discounts

**Business → Discounts** lets the verified owner create and edit percentage offers, use optional promo codes, target all items, one category or one specific service/product/workshop, set an expiry date (end of day in Trinidad) and deactivate an offer. Automatic offers require no code. Customer booking and checkout show a backend-validated quote before submitting; codes are case-insensitive and the best eligible percentage applies per item without stacking. Prices are snapshotted, delivery fees are excluded and booking deposits use the discounted total. **Apply discount** on unpaid pending appointment/order/enrollment records replaces the current discount and adjusts pending payment requests; it refuses records with received payments. Zero restores original prices. Demo workspace offers affect fictional records only.

Migrations 008–009 add discount storage, quoting and payment locking on the development branch. Deploy through the Supabase panel before production uses discounts. Owner authorization still requires the salon owner's account to be assigned in `lj_owners`.

## Received-revenue reports

**Business → Revenue & reports** provides current-month, 7-day, 30-day, year-to-date and custom-date reports in TTD using America/Port_of_Spain. Reports show receipts, actual refunds and net received, grouped by Services, Products and Workshops, with booked service/item details and receipt activity. Deposits count when received; pending and voided requests are excluded. Shared receipts are allocated to booked items in cents; delivery adjustments are separate. New records use receipt/refund timestamps; legacy request-date fallbacks are disclosed. These are cash-received reports, not profit-and-loss accounts.

Download CSV exports every matching item entry for the selected period/category, with receipt IDs, references and date basis, excluding client notes and card details. CSV cells escape quotes and neutralize text formulas. Web downloads directly; iOS/Android use Expo FileSystem and Sharing behind platform-specific modules. Finance source tables paginate past the default 1,000-row database limit. The labeled owner demo posts fictional receipts in memory and exports files visibly marked Demo data.

Migration 006 adds timestamped receipts, nullable customer linkage for walk-ins, voided deposit requests and owner-confirmed balance settlement on the development branch. The user must deploy it through the Supabase panel before production can use the new payment action.

## Signed service agreements

Booking Step 3 displays the supplied Client Service Agreement & Informed Consent and requires policy acceptance, client name and a drawn signature before continuing. The authenticated `lj-sign-agreement` Edge Function creates a PDF from immutable policy text, selected services, signature and server-stamped date; the private bucket permits reads only for the signing client and verified owner. Clients find downloads in Account → Service agreements; owners find them in Business → Signed service agreements and client records. `lj_book` locks and links a ready, matching, unused agreement before creating a reservation.

Migration 010 and the Edge Function are applied on the development branch. Deploy through the Supabase panel before production uses signed consent. Under-24-hour cancellations retain the deposit; any 50% future credit is discretionary. DOB collection does not send birthday gifts: a consent-aware messaging provider remains required.

Business → Specials combines existing active services into one bookable package with an owner-defined name, description and price. Its duration is calculated on the server; optional promotion places it on Home. Services use predefined category choices. Business → Apply discounts targets services, products, workshops or everything; personal pending-order discounts show the revised total and cannot change received payments.

Catalog editors support one or up to ten public photos with owner-only storage writes. Galleries support swiping and previous/next arrows. ## Daily assistance

Today’s bottom briefing follows the device clock to show remaining visits and suggested areas/supplies to prepare for the next client. Business holds the expandable attention list for bank verification, refunds, completed balances, paid orders, low stock, waitlists and email failures, with a prominent 8px red border pulsing every 900ms; reduced motion retains a solid outline. Business catalog modules are ordered Services, Products, Workshops. Product stock thresholds are editable, with zero disabling the alert. Maria focuses on its circular talk button and centered language controls, with explicit voice-not-connected status; OpenAI Realtime is planned, and no audio is recorded.

Clients can join up to ten date/service waitlists, check availability from My appointments, rebook completed services, update pending bank transfer references and cancel unpaid orders. Cancellation restores product stock or workshop capacity exactly once; received payments need separate owner reconciliation. Matching signed bookings close the waitlist entry.

Business → Expenses records paid salon spending, compares monthly net receipts with recorded expenses and exports CSV. This is a cash overview, not accounting profit. Business → Communication shows the transactional email queue and provider acceptance/failure history. Email delivery is paused by default; configure the verified sender and provider secrets as described in `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md`, then activate it separately in each environment. Migrations 013–015 and `lj-send-reminders` are development changes until deployed through the Supabase panel.

Owner contacts support private guardian information for clients under 18, inferred from DOB or marked when DOB is unknown. Delete contact requires confirmation and removes the directory entry while preserving the registered account and financial/appointment history. Profile synchronization does not recreate removed contacts. Appointment custom percentage discounts can reduce an unsettled confirmed visit after a deposit, but never change received receipts or lower the total below money received; settled appointments are protected. Migrations 016–017 must be deployed through the Supabase panel for production.

Calendar live synchronization uses a private-free `lj_calendar_availability` revision signal: owner views refetch actual rows and customer booking slots refresh immediately on changes and reconnect. Booking exclusion protection remains on the server. Deploy migrations **019–020** through the Supabase panel to enable the calendar features and Realtime in production.
